Comparing display panels, GEMS star ratings, and energy testing standards in Australia.

Australian Regulatory Certifications & Standards

Every television sold in Australia must comply with mandatory safety, electromagnetic, and energy efficiency standards before entering the market:

🎛️

ACMA & RCM Compliance

The Regulatory Compliance Mark (RCM) is mandated by the ACMA (equivalent to Malaysia's MCMC) and ERAC to ensure devices meet Australian electrical safety and radio interference (EMC) requirements.

🏷️

GEMS Regulator & MEPS

Under the Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (GEMS) Act 2012, all TVs must meet Minimum Energy Performance Standards (MEPS) and display the official Star Rating label.

📐

Calculation Standard (AS/NZS 62087)

Australian TV energy consumption figures are measured using AS/NZS 62087, calculated assuming a standardized duty cycle (On-Mode viewing + 0.5W–1W Standby mode).

Practical TV Energy Saving Tips

1. Enable Ambient Light Sensor

Automatic brightness sensors adjust the TV screen output depending on ambient room lighting, cutting energy usage by up to 25% at night.

2. Turn Off 'Quick Start' Standby

Quick-start features keep the TV internal processor active in standby, consuming 10W–15W continuously when not in use.

3. Check the GEMS Star Rating

Choosing a TV with 7 stars instead of 4 stars can save up to $180 over the typical 7-year lifespan of the television.
