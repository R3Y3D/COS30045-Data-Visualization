Storyboard & UI/UX Design Strategy

Mapping the narrative journey, user personas, and interface wireframes for the TV Energy Visualisation.

Target User Personas

Designing the data presentation around practical household decision-makers in the Australian market:

Sarah (Cost-Conscious Renter)

28 Years Old • Melbourne, VIC • Flat Tariff (34¢/kWh)

Goal: Wants to buy a 55" display for streaming movies without triggering steep quarterly power bill spikes.

Pain Point: Confused by technical terms like OLED vs. Mini-LED and cannot tell if star ratings actually save noticeable money.

David (Family Homeowner)

45 Years Old • Sydney, NSW • Time-of-Use Tariff

Goal: Upgrading the family living room TV that remains active 6+ hours daily for console gaming and TV broadcasting.

Pain Point: Unaware of standby power leakages and wants verifiable lifecycle cost savings.

Interactive Narrative Walkthrough

How a user navigates through the information hierarchy on the website:

[Top Bar: Brand Logo & Navigation]
[Hero: "The Hidden Cost of Screen Time"]
[Context Hook: 10% Household Electricity Draw]
Phase 1

The Context & Real-World Hook

The user arrives on the Televisions Data Story page. Rather than hitting an intimidating raw data table immediately, they encounter an accessible summary showing how modern screen sizes directly inflate electricity expenses under current Australian tariffs.

[Filter Controls: Screen Size / Display Tech]
[D3 Interactive Chart: Annual Cost ($AUD)]
[Hover Tooltips: kWh/year + Star Rating]
Phase 2

Exploration via Visualization

The user encounters the primary interactive visualization anchor. They can compare power draw differences between LED, QLED, OLED, and legacy Plasma displays, viewing costs translated into yearly Australian dollars rather than abstract Watt hours.

[Regulatory Standards: GEMS, RCM, ACMA]
[Interactive Calculator Form]
[Dynamic Results Output Panel]
Phase 3

Personalized Estimation

The user plugs in their specific viewing patterns (e.g., 4 hours/day) and their local tariff rate to generate an instant, customized cost projection, verified against official Australian test standards (AS/NZS 62087).

[Tips Grid: Sensor, Standby, Stars]
[Footer: Student Attribution & AI Declaration]
Phase 4

Actionable Conclusion

The page concludes with concrete power-saving actions (ambient light sensors, eliminating instant-on standby modes) that empower the consumer to apply their newfound insights immediately.
