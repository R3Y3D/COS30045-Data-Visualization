About The Project

Educational data storytelling on Australian residential appliance power use.

Project Purpose & Academic Context

The Appliance Energy Consumption Website was developed as an educational web development and data visualization project for Swinburne University of Technology. Its goal is to make household energy data accessible and directly actionable for everyday Australian consumers.
