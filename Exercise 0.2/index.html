Major Household Energy Consumers

Home appliances account for over 60% of average residential electricity bills. Here is how standard appliances compare in power usage:

🧊

Refrigerators

Avg. Wattage: 150 - 400 W

Runs 24/7 with compressor cycling. Energy Star models save up to 15% annually.

📺

Televisions

Avg. Wattage: 50 - 200 W

Power draw varies significantly by panel type (LED vs. OLED) and screen size.

View TV Breakdown →
❄️

Air Conditioners

Avg. Wattage: 1000 - 3500 W

The single highest energy consumer during summer months. Inverter ACs reduce peak load.

🧺

Washing Machines

Avg. Wattage: 500 - 1500 W

Heating water accounts for nearly 90% of the energy consumed during a cycle.
