Practical TV Energy Saving Tips

1. Enable Ambient Light Sensor

Automatic brightness sensors adjust the TV screen output depending on ambient room lighting, cutting energy usage by up to 25% at night.

2. Turn Off 'Quick Start' Standby

Quick start features keep the TV processor running continuously in low power mode, drawing up to 15W continuously when turned off.

3. Choose Energy Star Models

Look for Australian Energy Rating Labels with 6+ stars when purchasing new television sets.
